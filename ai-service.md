cd ai-service
pip install -r requirements.txt (This will install Ultralytics/YOLOv8, OpenCV, and Inquirer)
python live_camera.py
Use your arrow keys to select your webcam.
Put your phone or a backpack in front of the camera and don't touch it for 10 seconds. Check the Python console to see it upload the snapshot.
Open your Admin Snapshot Gallery in the browser, and you will see the exact picture the AI just took waiting for your review!
